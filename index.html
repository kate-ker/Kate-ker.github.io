Electrical & Computer Engineering

Kate Ker

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto (2022–2027). My work sits at the intersection of hardware, signal integrity, and functional safety: from 112G PAM4 Ethernet and PCIe Gen5 channels at Tenstorrent, to ISO 26262 safety validation at Rivian, to leading low-voltage architecture on the Formula Racing team. I care about designs that survive the lab and the vehicle, not just the schematic.
