TX Power Amplifier and Filter for Flexible Radio Transceiver

RF PCB design for a transmit chain: amplifier and filter work that was laid out in Altium, assembled, and measured into a real 50 Ω load.

Altium RF design PCB Soldering

Overview

This board applies RF design coursework to a flexible radio transceiver transmit path. The circuit was captured and laid out in Altium, then soldered and tested in hardware.

Results

  • Generated 1 W of continuous power into a 50 Ω load.
  • Input signal range of 8–16 MHz.
  • Hands-on bring-up after assembly, not just schematic-level analysis.
