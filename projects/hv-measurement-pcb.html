HV Measurement Point PCB

A safety-critical measurement board designed in Altium. Isolation, indication, and a discharge path are the core of the design.

Altium PCB Optocoupler Functional safety

Overview

This PCB acts as a safety-critical sensor at a high-voltage measurement point. An optocoupler is used to determine the measured voltage while keeping the indicator and control side isolated from the HV node.

Functions

  • Voltage sensing through an optocoupler.
  • LED indication of measured state.
  • Relay control to a discharge resistor.
